O

The Fool

New beginnings, innocence, spontaneity, free spirit

Recklessness, naivety, foolishness, negligence

The player upon starting a new save file, this reverses if the player dies during the first 2 minutes of their run

3

I

The Magician

Manifestation, resourcefulness, power, inspired action

Manipulation, poor planning, untapped talents, trickery

This is shown when offering a stats upgrade, and can occasionally be reversed to secretly reduce stats

4

II

The High Priestess

Intuition, sacred knowledge, divine feminine, subconscious mind

Secrets, disconnected from intuition, withdrawal, repressed feelings

When most of the map has been explored and the mini-map has been unlocked, this card will be drawn and the rest of the map will be revealed, the reversed symbol of the card will show surrounding areas of interest that have not been explored

5

III

The Empress

Femininity, beauty, nature, nurturing, abundance

Creative block, dependence on others, neglect, smothering

An upgrade that allows one free revive during a run. This can occasionally reverse and revives the player without any weapons/currency

6

IV

The Emperor

Authority, establishment, structure, father figure

Domination, excessive control, lack of discipline, tyranny

An upgrade that allows some currency to be retained in new runs, they also have the option to reverse the card and regain all money from the previous run at the expense of not being able to use the upright card for the next 3 runs

7

V

The Hierophant

Spiritual wisdom, religious beliefs, conformity, tradition

Personal beliefs, freedom, challenging the status quo, dogma

The log of all discovered weapons and abilties plus their descriptions (in a menu somewhere idk)

8

VI

The Lovers

Love, harmony, values alignment, choices

Self-love, disharmony, imbalance, misaligned values, indecision

An upgrade that allows you to split into two halves and move/fight mirrored (each half has the same health as the player and damage is split according to the two weapons the player has picked up). The card is reversed if one of these halves takes damage and the player returns to one object, with their health halved

9

VII

The Chariot

Control, willpower, success, action, determination

Lack of control, aggression, lack of direction, coercion

Increased health, grants invulnerability after hit

10

VIII

Strength

Strength, courage, persuasion, influence, compassion

Self-doubt, weakness, low energy, lack of confidence, insecurity

A strength upgrade, allows for dual-wield

11

IX

The Hermit

Soul searching, introspection, being alone, inner guidance

Isolation, loneliness, withdrawal, anti-social, lost

This is a teleportation ability that works when not in combat, when interacting with an appropriate object the player will teleport to any area they have already explored. If the area they teleport to has enemies present, they will experience a blindness effect and the card will be reversed

12

X

Wheel of Fortune

Good luck, karma, life cycles, destiny, turning point

Bad luck, resistance to change, breaking cycles, setbacks

This could represent a literal wheel of fortune, or some kind of chest

13

XI

Justice

Justice, fairness, truth, cause and effect, law

Unfairness, lack of accountability, dishonesty, biased judgment

This can represent the ending of the game, upright or reversed depending on which one

14

XII

The Hanged Man

Pause, surrender, letting go, new perspectives

Delays, resistance, stalling, indecision, pointless sacrifice

The area between levels (or maybe some major decision point)

15

XIII

Death

Endings, change, transformation, transition

Resistance to change, personal transformation, inner purging, stagnation

When the player dies this card could be shown, if they have an ability that grants a revive, it can show reversed

16

XIV

Temperance

Balance, moderation, patience, purpose

Imbalance, excess, self-healing, realignment, disharmony

Allows the player to redistribute their stat points once - this could technically result in either the upright or reversed card depending on their allocations

17

XV

The Devil

Shadow self, attachment, addiction, restriction, sexuality

Releasing limiting beliefs, exploring dark thoughts, detachment, reclaiming power

The main boss of the game, this card is reversed upon defeating him

18

XVI

The Tower

Sudden change, upheaval, chaos, revelation, awakening

Personal transformation, fear of change, averting disaster, delayed collapse

The final stage of the game that houses the boss, reversed until completion

19

XVII

The Star

Hope, faith, purpose, renewal, spirituality

Lack of faith, despair, disconnection, hopelessness

Level 3 (reversed until completion)

20

XVIII

The Moon

Illusion, fear, the unconscious, intuition

Release of fear, repressed emotion, inner confusion, deception revealed

Level 2 (reversed until completion - the upright is also negative so this will act as some form of plot twist*)

21

XIX

The Sun

Positivity, fun, warmth, success, vitality

Sadness, pessimism, feeling down, overly optimistic, temporary darkness

Level 1 (reversed until completion)

22

XX

Judgement

Judgment, rebirth, inner calling, absolution

Self-doubt, inner critic, ignoring the call, harsh self-judgment

This is shown when the player restarts after death, and is reversed if the player goes back to the menu instead of replaying

23

XXI

The World

Completion, integration, accomplishment, travel

Seeking personal closure, short-cuts, delays, incomplete goals

The minimap is unlocked when this card is drawn
