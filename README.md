# Coursework Weapons and Combat
Brief introduction
In my project, I want a wide range of weapons in terms of damage, speed, range and AOE, plus additional unique effects applied to all so every weapon feels different to use and can encourage a new playstyle. I also want each weapon to be visually unique so a player can recognise weapons that they like from a distance even when not hovering over with their mouse. I want each weapon to have some basic description, basic stat breakdown and active effects shown on screen when a player hovers over it and is standing near that weapon. I may also potentially add an option to see a detailed statistical breakdown in a side menu. I want the player to be able to wield 1-2 weapons, with standard weapons taking up 1 slot and heavy weapons taking up 2. If the player chooses to wield 2 of the same weapon, I want to implement an akimbo move-set with potentially unique and additional moves. I think that limiting the second weapon slot to the first introductory upgrade will be a good step to encourage the player to push for completion and will be rewarding when unlocked. I think it would be a good idea to feature multiple distinct tiers of weapon, so that scaling can be applied on a wider scale and the player has a general sense of the power of their weapon if they haven't used it before
